Privacy policy for wörds

Last updated: 2026-10-23

wörds is a flashcard app for learning languages, provided by anmx, anmx-dev@no.spam.pm.me ("we").

In short

wörds does not collect personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Data stored on your device

  • Your learning progress (your answers and the review schedule derived from them), study sessions and settings
  • The language packs you install, including their pictures and audio
  • The GitHub sources you add and, for private repositories, your access token. The token is encrypted with a key held in your device's secure hardware (Android Keystore). It is shown again only after you confirm with your screen lock, and it is never included in backups.

You can delete this data at any time: reset your learning progress in the settings, remove sources and packs, or uninstall the app.

Connections to GitHub

wörds connects to the internet only to read language packs from GitHub repositories that you add yourself. The app then sends requests to GitHub (api.github.com), with your access token for private repositories. GitHub receives the technical data of these requests, such as your IP address. Its handling is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We receive none of this data. If you add no GitHub source, the app makes no network connections at all.

Backup

If backup is enabled on your device, Android includes your learning progress and settings in your device backup. Access tokens and pack media are excluded. Backups are managed by your device and your Google account, not by us.

Pronunciation and biometrics

The pronunciation uses the speech voices installed on your device. Confirming access to a saved token uses your device's screen lock or biometric prompt. The app never receives biometric data.

Children

wörds is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
